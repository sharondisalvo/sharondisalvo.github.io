Approach

Two very different sites. The same way of working.

  • I listen before I design

    The goal isn't to make you fit my aesthetic. It's to understand yours, and the people you want to reach.

  • Words first

    Most practitioners find saying what they do harder than choosing how it looks. So we start with your words: your voice, your vision, how you'd describe your work to someone you trust. Then I design around them, so the site feels like you.

  • No platform, no template

    No Wix, Squarespace or ready-made themes. Each site is designed and coded individually, so it's secure, tailored to your practice down to the details, and authentically yours.

  • I stay after launch

    I can host it, maintain it, keep it secure and updated, and change it as your practice grows. You stay the practitioner; you don't become the webmaster.
